# Phase 8: Privacy and data controls

Primary target: `bakibuddy/src/app/data-privacy.tsx`. Related target: Settings. Mode: Operate. Inherit the Daily Planner design, existing form navigation, scalable typography and 48-unit controls.

## Direction contract

THESIS: People can take a readable copy of their records and deliberately remove the local ledger, with consequences visible before either action.

OWN-WORLD: Existing warm off-white column, navy text, Manrope headings, fine rules, emerald actions and orange destructive confirmation. No new token, card hierarchy, motion system or identity.

STORY: See current record counts, choose an export with its exact scope, then open the system destination chooser. Deletion lives behind a separate review that names affected data and leaves external copies untouched.

FIRST VIEWPORT: FormScreen back control and Privacy & data title, local-storage summary and current counts, followed by export choices. A separated deletion section follows exports. Review replaces the task content with consequences, acknowledgement, typed DELETE and explicit cancel/confirm controls. Status and errors appear adjacent to their action.

FORM: Specified extension of Settings/form patterns; no concept seed or approved comp applies. Unencrypted exports and unavailable restore are disclosed before export. No automatic export or deletion occurs.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
